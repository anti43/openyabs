    DATEV export (Booking batch, Master data & Document package)¶

    Deutsch: DATEV-Export

    The DATEV export plugin (datev.jar, version 2.0.0, requires OpenYaBS 2.0.23+) provides three DATEV-compliant exports from the Journal for DATEV Kanzlei-Rechnungswesen and DATEV Unternehmen online:

    1. DATEV-Buchungsstapel (CSV) – DATEV-Format EXTF, header version 700, format category 21, format version 13 (125 columns), including automatic integration into the tax-adviser ZIP package.
    2. DATEV-Stammdaten Debitoren/Kreditoren (CSV) – DATEV-Format EXTF, header version 700, format category 16, format version 5 (51 columns).
    3. DATEV-Belegpaket (ZIP) – DATEV XML-Schnittstelle online (version 6.0) for Belege online / DATEV Belegtransfer with posting metadata and original invoice files (PDF/ZUGFeRD or XRechnung).

    Installation¶

    1. Import datev.jar from the plugins/ folder of the OpenYaBS distribution (or openyabs-plugins-<version>.zip) via Settings › Plugins › Import plugin (requires OpenYaBS 2.0.23 or newer).
    2. Import a chart of accounts (if not done yet) via Settings › Accounts › Import using the updated files in ext/:
    3. ext/accounts-german-skr03.xml – SKR03 (cleaned up: outdated 16 % accounts removed, German UStVA field numbers updated, e.g. 81 instead of 51, GWG account 0480 properly labeled),
    4. ext/accounts-german-skr04.xml – SKR04.

    Settings¶

    Under Settings › Plugins › DATEV-Export (stored per database under the datev.* prefix; if settings have not been saved yet, exports automatically use the defaults for the detected chart of accounts and show a reminder in the export dialog):

    • Header metadata & fiscal year:
    • Consultant number (Beraternummer) (1001–9999999) and client number (Mandantennummer) (1–99999).
    • Fiscal year start (Wirtschaftsjahresbeginn) (YYYYMMDD, e.g. 20260101) – supports deviating fiscal years (e.g. 20250701) without false cross-year warnings; invalid calendar dates are validated.
    • Account length (Sachkontenlänge) (4–8, typically 4) – class-0 accounts (e.g. 0480, 0670) are properly left-padded with leading zeros; account numbers exceeding the configured length are reported as errors.
    • Chart of accounts (Kontenrahmen) (03 = SKR03, 04 = SKR04, prefilled from imported accounts).
    • Batch label (Bezeichnung) (max 30 chars), optional initials (Diktatkürzel) (1–2 uppercase letters), lock on import (Festschreibung) (off by default so the tax adviser can review before locking).
    • Document date & date of service:
    • Document date mode: Payment date (cash basis / EÜR) (default) or Document date (invoice date).
    • Export date of service / tax period (columns 115–116, § 14 Abs. 4 Nr. 6 UStG): enabled by default. Whenever the delivery/service date differs from the booking date, both Leistungsdatum (DDMMYYYY) and the tax period assignment date are exported for clean period cutoffs and VAT rate transitions.
    • Money accounts: income and expense money accounts (default 1200 for SKR03, 1800 for SKR04), plus an optional cash account (e.g. 1000 / 1600) for cash register (POS) sales.
    • Automatic accounts & BU tax keys:
    • Export DATEV automatic accounts (AM/AV) without BU key (enabled by default): the BU key is omitted strictly for official DATEV AM/AV automatic accounts (in SKR03/SKR04, e.g. 8400/8300/3400/3300 or 4400/4300/5400/5300) and any optionally configured client-specific automatic accounts.
    • Regular non-automatic accounts (e.g. SKR03 4930 office supplies, 4600 advertising, 0480 GWG) always emit the appropriate BU key (9/8 for 19 %/7 % expenses, 3/2 for 19 %/7 % income), preventing both double taxation and missing input VAT.
    • Accounts for 0 % sales by VAT category & small business exemption (§ 19 UStG): The category is taken from the line items' tax rate (customer rate, product rate, or default tax rate):
    • Tax-free domestic sales (0 %, no special category / category E): 8100 (SKR03) or 4100 (SKR04). When the small business VAT exemption (§ 19 UStG) is enabled in company settings (Small business VAT exemption), 8195 (SKR03) or 4185 (SKR04) is used automatically without a BU key.
    • 0 % EU delivery account (category K, intra-community supply, § 4 Nr. 1b UStG): 8125 (SKR03) or 4125 (SKR04).
    • EU services, reverse charge (category AE, customer with a VAT ID of another EU country, § 3a Abs. 2 UStG): 8336 or 4336 (UStVA line 21, not line 41).
    • § 13b UStG domestic reverse charge (category AE without an EU VAT ID): 8337 or 4337.
    • Exports outside the EU (category G, § 4 Nr. 1a UStG): 8120 or 4120.
    • Invoices with mixed 0 % categories are split cleanly by category. For 0 % EU sales, the contact's EU VAT ID (including GR → EL normalization for Greece) is written to column 40 (EU-Land u. UStID) for the EC sales list.
    • Document package: Invoice folder in Belege online (default: Ausgangsrechnungen).
    • Accounts & BU keys per tax rate: fallback revenue and expense accounts and BU keys per tax rate, with a Defaults from chart of accounts button.

    1. DATEV booking batch (Buchungsstapel, CSV)¶

    Available via Journal › Export › DATEV-Buchungsstapel (CSV) or directly for the current year via Plugins › DATEV: Buchungsstapel exportieren (aktuelles Jahr).

    • Complete & independent of journal filters: reloads all documents of the selected period (by payment date or document date as configured in DATEV settings). Active search or table filters in the Journal do not affect the export.
    • Preview & validation dialog: before saving, the dialog displays the period, file name, selection rule, client metadata, totals (debits, credits, balance, line count), a full table of all bookings (including service date, currency, account/offset account, BU key, EU VAT ID), and a second Not exported table stating the exact reason for each omitted document (e.g. open, cancelled, paid in another period).
    • Blocking error checks: errors that would cause DATEV import failures or invalid bookings (such as missing/invalid consultant or client number, booking date outside the batch period, account number exceeding configured length, document number exceeding 36 characters, or foreign currency without exchange rate/base amount) block saving. In the tax-adviser ZIP package, an invalid batch is rejected with an error message rather than bundled silently.
    • File format & encoding: EXTF_Buchungsstapel_<YYYYMMDD>_<YYYYMMDD>.csv in Windows-1252, CRLF, semicolon-separated, decimal comma, quoted text. Text is NFC-normalized and safely sanitized for Windows-1252 (umlauts in document numbers are transliterated rather than dropped; control characters and non-CP1252 emojis are safely stripped).
    • Booking rules:
    • Exports paid, booking-relevant records only (invoices, deposit/part-payment invoices, credit notes, income, expenses, and individual partial payments with their payment date) – never cancelled or unpaid documents.
    • Multi-rate documents (including line and overall discounts) and partial payments are split exact to the cent by tax rate and 0 % VAT category.
    • Credit notes and cancellation invoices cleanly reverse the debit/credit indicator (H) without double sign inversion.
    • Foreign currencies: exports WKZ Umsatz, Kurs, Basis-Umsatz, and WKZ Basis-Umsatz (columns 3–6) per the EXTF specification.
    • GoBD traceability & export audit log:
    • Every booking line carries a deterministic Buchungs-GUID (column 103) and, when available, a Beleglink (column 20, BEDI UUID) and Beleginfo linking back to the original document.
    • Completed exports are recorded in the export audit log (file name, period, line count, SHA-256 checksum, and exported line keys). Re-exporting previously exported booking lines triggers a duplicate-import warning in the dialog.
    • Tax-adviser ZIP package: while the plugin is active, the booking batch CSV is automatically added under datev/EXTF_Buchungsstapel_<YYYYMMDD>_<YYYYMMDD>.csv (when error-free).

    2. DATEV master data Debitoren/Kreditoren (CSV)¶

    Available via Journal › Export › DATEV-Stammdaten Debitoren/Kreditoren (CSV).

    • Format: DATEV-Format EXTF 700, format category 16, format version 5, Windows-1252, CRLF, semicolon-separated, 51 columns.
    • File name: EXTF_Stammdaten_Debitoren_Kreditoren_<YYYYMMDD>.csv.
    • Contents: exports customers (debtors / Debitoren, e.g. 10000–69999) and suppliers (creditors / Kreditoren, e.g. 70000–99999) with account numbers of length Sachkontenlänge + 1, proper addressee type (Adressattyp) (1 = natural person, 2 = company), names, postal address, EU country & VAT ID (including EL for Greece), contact details, and bank account data (IBAN, SWIFT/BIC with the mandatory empty column 46).

    3. DATEV document package (XML-Schnittstelle online, with e-invoices)¶

    Available via Journal › Export › DATEV-Belegpaket (XML-Schnittstelle online, mit E-Rechnungen).

    • Format & target: generates a ZIP archive DATEV_Belege_<YYYYMMDD>_<YYYYMMDD>.zip following the official DATEV XSD schemas of XML-Schnittstelle online (version 6.0) for DATEV Unternehmen online / Belege online (uploaded via DATEV Belegtransfer into the configured invoice folder, default Ausgangsrechnungen).
    • Selection: all outgoing invoices, deposit/part-payment invoices, and credit notes whose invoice date falls in the selected period – regardless of payment status or Journal filters. Cancelled invoices are exported only together with their cancellation credit note (otherwise omitted and listed in the dialog).
    • ZIP contents:
    • document.xml (document index),
    • one ledger XML file per invoice/credit note (accountsReceivableLedger, Rechnungsdaten_RA_….xml / Rechnungskorrektur_RAK_….xml) with the exact same tax-rate split, revenue accounts, and BU keys as the booking batch, plus invoice date, service date, and (when paid) payment date,
    • one document file per invoice: prefers the archived original (PDF, ZUGFeRD, or XRechnung, e.g. archived by the GoBD plugin); for e-invoice customers without an archived original, includes the generated XRechnung XML (never a second diverging "original").
    • Validation & DATEV limits: uses the same preview and validation dialog (net/VAT/gross totals, document list with accounts and attached files, and Not exported table). Files over 20 MB (DATEV limit) are reported; packages exceeding DATEV's document count limit or 100 MB trigger warnings or block export.
    • Note: built according to the official DATEV sample files and XSD schemas (v6.0), not certified by DATEV – please review the first import together with your tax adviser.

    Importing into DATEV¶

    • Do not open and re-save the CSV files in Excel (Excel alters the header, delimiters, leading zeros, and number formatting).
    • DATEV Kanzlei-Rechnungswesen (booking batch & master data): import via Bestand › Importieren › Stapelverarbeitung.
    • DATEV Unternehmen online (document package ZIP): upload via DATEV Belegtransfer to the configured folder (default Ausgangsrechnungen).
