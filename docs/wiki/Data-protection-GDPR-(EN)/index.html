    Data protection (GDPR) and retention duties¶

    Deutsch: Datenschutz (DSGVO)

    Customers can ask for their data to be erased (Art. 17 GDPR) or for a copy (Art. 15, Art. 20 GDPR). At the same time invoices and other records must be kept. YaBS combines both.

    What must be kept¶

    Records Period Law
    Issued invoices, credit notes, part payment and deposit invoices, booked income 8 years § 147 AO, § 14b UStG (since 2025)
    Business letters: orders, order confirmations, delivery notes, accepted offers, activity lists, purchase orders 6 years § 147 AO, § 257 HGB
    • The period starts at the end of the calendar year of the document. Example: invoice of 15.03.2026 → keep until 31.12.2034.
    • While a tax assessment is still open, the period can be longer (§ 147 Abs. 3 AO). Ask your tax adviser if in doubt.
    • The documents must not be changed (GoBD). So the name and addresses of the contact, which the documents show, stay as well.
    • The right to erasure does not apply to this data (Art. 17 Abs. 3 lit. b GDPR). It is blocked (restriction of processing, Art. 18): used only for the retention, e.g. a tax audit.
    • Drafts (invoices never issued) and offers that were not accepted need not be kept.

    Deleting a contact¶

    Open the contact > Delete.

    Without documents to keep: as before. The contact goes to the trash with its drafts and offers.

    With documents to keep: the contact can not be deleted. A dialog shows:

    • Deleted now: phone, fax, email, website, notes, bank details, conversations, files, appointments, web shop links and own fields of the contact, offers not accepted and draft invoices.
    • Kept unchanged and blocked: name, customer number, addresses and VAT ID, and all documents that must be kept, each with the end of its period. The data needed to print the documents again also stays (Leitweg-ID, Peppol-ID, EORI number, Skonto, payment account).
    • Open invoices (issued, neither paid nor cancelled): book the payment or cancel them first. An open claim is a reason to keep the data (Art. 17 Abs. 3 lit. e). Until then, blocking is not possible.

    Buttons:

    • Export personal data… – first, if the person asked for a copy (see below).
    • Block and delete the rest – deletes as described above and blocks the contact until the end of the last period. This is recorded in the activity log. It can not be undone.

    A blocked contact is marked red in the contact view ("Blocked (GDPR)"). No new documents can be created for it.

    After the retention period¶

    Trash > Retention over lists the blocked contacts whose documents need not be kept any more, and after asking deletes them for good with all documents. The delete dialog of the contact then offers "Delete for good" as well.

    Trash¶

    • Issued invoices within their period and contacts with documents to keep are not deleted for good when the trash is emptied. YaBS says why. Before, the database deleted all invoices of a contact together with it.
    • Records in the trash keep their own fields (properties), so they can be restored completely. These are deleted only when the trash is emptied.

    Right of access (Art. 15, Art. 20)¶

    Open the contact > Export personal data… writes everything stored about the person to a ZIP file:

    • one JSON file per kind of data: contact, addresses, documents, positions, payments, reminders, income, conversations, activity lists, purchase orders, appointments, own fields;
    • the files of the contact (files/);
    • the files written for the documents (PDF, e-invoice) in documents/, if they still exist.

    The file contains personal data: hand it over securely and delete your own copy afterwards. The export is recorded in the activity log.

    Note: this is guidance, not legal advice.
